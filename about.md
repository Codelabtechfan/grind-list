# Grind List

Keep track of the online levels you are grinding!

- A new **Grinding** button on the main menu, right next to the online levels button.
- See every level you're grinding with its difficulty, best normal %, practice % and attempts.
- Tap **+** to search online levels with all the normal filters and add them to your list.
- Every online level page gets a **+** button to add it to (or remove it from) your grind list.
